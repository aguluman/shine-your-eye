# Voiceover script

Final cut: `~/Videos/shine-demo-silent.mp4` — 1:32, silent, captions burned in.
Record your voice over it. You can also record each block separately and send me
the files; I will place them at these timestamps and normalise the audio.

## How to read it

- Speak **Nigerian English**, conversational. Talk to one person, not an audience.
- Do not read like a news anchor or an advert. Let some sentences fall.
- Silence is part of the performance. When a pause is written, take it.
- Slow down for the redaction and consent lines. Speed up only for the scam catch.
- Drop your volume, do not raise it, for "Nothing left this machine" and "never
  left the phone". Intimacy lands harder than drama.
- Smile with your voice on "This is Shine your eye". Go flat and serious on the
  scam lines.
- Avoid uptalk at the end of sentences. Land them.

## Recording setup

- Quiet room, soft furnishings, no fan. Phone voice memo is fine; laptop mic is
  fine if you stay 20–30 cm away and slightly off-axis.
- Record **one block at a time**. Name the files `01-intro`, `02-redaction`,
  `03-ondevice`, `04-trail`, `05-scam`, `06-offline`, `07-android`,
  `08-consent`, `09-verdict`, `10-close`.
- Clap once at the start of each file. That gives me a sync marker.
- Keep the same distance and tone across blocks.
- Send me the files, or record one continuous pass while watching the silent
  video and following the timestamps.
- If you add music, keep it under the voice and below −24 dB. I would leave it
  out; the silence is stronger.
